## 1. Information we collect

**You give us**
- Account data: username, password (stored only as a one-way hash, so nobody, including us, can read it), name, email address, language, and preferences.
- Your use of the Service: the readings you draw (cards, positions, dates), the questions you type, favorites, and settings such as when you want a daily message from our bot.
- Subscription and promo data: your plan, promo codes you redeem, and the bonuses they give.
- Messages to support.
- If you connect a Telegram bot: your Telegram user ID and username, and your messages to the bot.

**We collect automatically**
- Time zone, taken from your browser so we can send messages at the right local time. We do not collect your location.
- Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, pages and actions, timestamps, error logs.
- Cookies and similar storage: a session cookie to keep you signed in and local storage in your browser for preferences such as color theme.

**From payment providers**
Payments are handled by a payment provider ([PAYMENT PROVIDER]). We receive the status of your payment, plan, amount, and a customer identifier. We do not receive or store your full card number.

## 2. How we use it
To run your account and the Service; to create and save your readings and show them to you; to produce AI interpretations; to send daily cards and notices you asked for; to process payments and prevent fraud and abuse; to provide support; to keep the Service secure and fix errors; to understand how the Service is used and improve it; to comply with law; and to send service emails (confirmation, password reset, receipts, renewal reminders). We send marketing emails only if you agree, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

## 3. Artificial intelligence
When you ask for an AI interpretation, we send only the cards drawn, their positions, and the text of any question you typed to an AI provider ([AI PROVIDER], currently DeepSeek) to generate the text. We do not send your name, email address, username, account number, or Telegram identifier. The AI provider may process this data on servers outside the United States and under its own terms. Please do not type sensitive personal information, or information about other people, in your questions. We do not use your questions to build advertising profiles.
